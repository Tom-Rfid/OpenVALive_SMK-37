# Contributing

Thank you! Issues and pull requests in English or French are welcome. The rules: [docs/_EN/18](docs/_EN/18-rules-git-and-publishing.md)
([français](docs/_FR/18-regles-git-et-publication.md)).

## Most useful right now

1. **Run the test plan on a real SMK-37** (Elite or Pro): [02](docs/_EN/02-the-smk37.md#test-plan-the-elite-first).
2. Report what the T-8 (or another drum machine) does on MIDI OUT.

## Where does my change go?

| Change | Repository |
|---|---|
| the sound, an engine, the Live Core, the screens and pages | [OpenVALive_CORE](https://github.com/Tom-Rfid/OpenVALive_CORE) (MIT; then the `va/` pin moves here) |
| the SMK-37's controls, panel, audio, storage, build, flash scripts | here (GPL-3.0) |

Contributions here are GPL-3.0 with `smk37_open`'s linking exception. Never write the calibration or the pairing.
