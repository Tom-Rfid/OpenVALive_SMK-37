A live-first, open-source firmware for the M-VAVE SMK-37 Elite and Pro.
Four looping tracks, a virtual analog synth and five drum kits, played like an instrument: no menus on stage.

Download (preview) Guide Source

What it does

  • Four tracks, one clock — synths or drums, each with its own loop; swing; MIDI clock in and out, and the MIDI OUT jack drives a Roland T-8.
  • CAPTURE and LOOP EDIT — play freely, press once: it loops, in time. Then edit the loop note by note.
  • 29 sounds in 7 families — acid, basses, drum kits (808, 909…), leads and keys, pads, textures, your own.
  • The pads — PERFORM effects, PLAY (drum rolls by pressure) or SCENES (recall, store, mutes).
  • The 37 keys on screen while a layer button is held: what each key does now.
  • Scenes and Morph, a Creative Engine, undo everything.
